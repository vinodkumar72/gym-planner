# App Store screenshots

Screenshots must be real captures of the app. Capture them on an iPhone running the TestFlight build
(Expo Go captures are acceptable for a first submission but the TestFlight build shows the real icon and splash).

## Required sizes (App Store Connect, 2026)

| Device class | Resolution (portrait) | Example device |
|---|---|---|
| 6.9" / 6.7" iPhone | 1320 × 2868 or 1290 × 2796 | iPhone 16 Pro Max / 15 Pro Max |
| 6.5" iPhone | 1284 × 2778 or 1242 × 2688 | iPhone 11 Pro Max |

Upload 3 to 10 per size. If you only have one phone, capture on it and let App Store Connect scale
(it accepts the larger size and reuses it for the smaller slot).

## Preparation (once)

1. Install the app on the phone, finish onboarding (pick Push / Pull / Legs for the best-looking charts).
2. Settings → Data → **Load sample data** (only shown while history is empty). This creates eight weeks of
   realistic, progressive workouts through the normal workout pipeline.
3. Switch to the look you want in Settings → Appearance (dark looks best on the store page).
4. Turn on Do Not Disturb and charge above 50% so the status bar is clean.

## Shots to take (press Side + Volume Up on iPhone)

| # | Screen | How to get there | Suggested caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Today | Today tab, with the readiness chip set to "Ready" | "Know what to train today" |
| 2 | Active workout | Start Workout, complete two sets so one row is green and the rest timer is visible | "Log a set with one tap" |
| 3 | Suggestion detail | Same screen, scroll so an exercise card shows "Last time" and "Suggested" | "Smart weight progression, explained" |
| 4 | Workout summary | Finish the workout | "See what to do next time" |
| 5 | Progress | Progress tab | "Track trends, records and streaks" |
| 6 | Exercise trend | Progress → tap Bench Press | "Watch your lifts climb" |
| 7 | History | History tab with the calendar | "Every workout, on your phone only" |
| 8 | Routines | Routines tab | "Built-in and custom routines" |
| 9 | Routine editor | Routines → Push / Pull / Legs → Push Day | "Customise every set and rep range" |
| 10 | Backup | Settings → Backup & restore | "Your data stays yours" |

## Polishing (optional)

Device frames and captions are not required by Apple. If you want them, free tools that accept raw
captures: Apple's own App Store Connect preview, `screenshots.pro`, or Figma's "App Store Screenshot"
community templates. Keep captions short and avoid text that overlaps the status bar.

## After capturing

AirDrop or sync the PNGs to a computer, then drag them into App Store Connect → your app → the version
page → "App Previews and Screenshots". Do not resize PNGs with a tool that re-compresses them (JPEG is
rejected).
